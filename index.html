About Akan Day Name Calculator

This application calculates your Akan day name based on the date and gender you provide. The Akan day names are based on the traditional naming system from the Akan people of Ghana, which assigns a name to each day of the week.

The day name is determined by the day of the week you were born on. For example, if you were born on a Monday, your Akan name would be either "Kwadwo" (for males) or "Adwoa" (for females).

Enter your birthdate and gender, and the application will show you your Akan day name!
